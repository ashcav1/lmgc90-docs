# Legacy: everything before 2 November 2026

Before the three standard beds, the study tried six beds of increasing realism,
many test beds and several drop sets. It taught us what the standard beds are
built on, but none of those beds or drops are part of the results now.

**What it taught us, in short:**

- Beds blew apart until a speed limit was applied on every step.
- Boulder pairs need the position rule, or they overlap.
- A drop must end at the exact moment no part of the craft can touch the ground
  again, and the craft with its panels open must turn about its true centre of mass.
- Small stones poured on coarse boulders fall into the gaps.
- A bug in the pour filled only 83 % of the box for boulders from 20 cm long, so
  those beds came out tilted 4-9° and took days to settle. It is fixed, and every
  bed report now shows its tilt.

**Where it all is:**

- The pages in this section of the menu: the old plan, the old status page, every
  old run, the dated lab notes, the old six-bed study and the decision log.
- In the code repository, the folder `legacy/`: old configs, every report from both
  machines, the old overnight scripts and notes.
- On each machine, `runs/legacy/`: the simulation data itself (too big for GitHub).
- The old drop reel's 240 drops are kept on the documentation repository's
  `drops-media-legacy` branch.
