# Failure modes: what goes wrong, how it shows, how to avoid it

_Started 2026-10-29 from the first study night and the laptop
experiments that followed; each entry is something that actually happened.
Add to it whenever a run fails for a new reason. The QA checks in
`milani/pipeline.py` and the settle invariants in `milani/beds.py` turn most
of these into hard failures with a message, so a silent wrong result is the
failure to fear most — several below used to be silent._

## Settling beds

| what happened | how it showed | cause | avoid / detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 beds settled all night, none finished | every window still "depositing" at 07:00; 20–30 s per step | grains were deposited as proxy spheroids: a 6 m column for a 1.2 m layer, 29k contacts × 2–4k NLGS iterations per step, 16 processes sharing the Mac | real-shape deposition (`deposit_polyhedra`, 64 candidates: column 1.7× the layer); one bed per window; `lab.py estimate` before a slate |
| thick bed never reaches "every grain < 2 mm/s" | p99 speed 0.1–0.3 m/s for ever, 130–260 of 463 boulders "moving", no overlap, no escape, solver converged | a pile several grains deep chatters: the common-plane contact set flickers ±5 contacts per 50 steps and re-kicks the pile; thin beds damp it, thick ones don't | rest is structural (`rest_disp_m`: median displacement over 1 s < 1 mm); `n_moving`, `d50/d95` columns in `settle_metrics.csv` show it |
| bed at rest explodes within a second | 150–355 boulders at 0.6–3.4 m/s right after `capped` turns 0 | the speed limit was lifted; uncapped contact dynamics of a deep polyhedral pile are unstable (clip is inert at rest, yet removing it blows the pile up — four times, any solver budget) | never lift the cap; the final phase only switches to the full solver |
| bed at rest explodes at release (variant) | same, with `deposit_dt` ≠ `dt` | the time step changed mid-settle (stored reactions scale with dt) | one dt per settle (`deposit_dt` removed) |
| thick top layer of small stones layer fails QA on overlap | `overlap_rel_fines FAIL 0.84` after a clean settle (2 m bed, 10 cm of small stones 8–16 cm long on boulders); a 25 cm pile of the same small stones on the floor only warns (0.35) | the speed rule drifts small stones into each other; a thicker small-stone layer drifts further than the 3 cm smoke thin top layer (0.2–0.4, WARN) | beds 4–6 are exposed. The failing overlap is the boulder–stone pairs (−20 mm); stone–stone pairs sit at −8 mm (WARN, like the small-stones-only beds, which pass). The position rule for *all* small-stone pairs removes the overlap on small-stones-only beds (QA PASS; 2.7× the step cost thin, ~9× for a 25 cm pile) but on small stones poured over boulders it ran 5,800 steps at 4–6 s/step (10×) without resting — the small stones flicker between the boulders. Next: `[settle] boulder_fines_law = "GAP_SGR_CLB"` (position rule for boulder–stone pairs only, speed rule among small stones; `configs/experiments/fines_split.toml`). A failed bed is rejected, its 36 drops do not run |
| velocity-law boulders fail QA | `overlap_rel_boulders FAIL 0.196` (limit 0.10) after a clean settle | RST_CLB only stops approach velocity; dense polyhedra drift 5–16 mm into each other | boulders settle under GAP_SGR_CLB (position rule, ~0 overlap); RST stays for small-stone pairs (gap-law small stones drain through pores) |
| settle crashes with NaN | `nlgs_diverged: a NaN appeared in contact reactions`, stage retried | friction 0.9 with the position rule on rigid polyhedra: the NLGS diverged | keep settle friction ≤ 0.6–0.7 (Dimorphos angle of repose 30–35° → μ 0.55–0.7); the crash detector names it; one retry with the same settings |
| bed looks settled but every boulder lies on the floor | surface 5.6 cm for a 17 cm layer, `POLYR-POLYR` contacts = 0, QA only warned | face-to-face detection (`PRPRx_UseCpF2fDetection` / `...Explicit`) finds no contacts on triangulated random hulls | Cundall common-plane detection only; QA now FAILS on < 1 grain–grain contact per grain or a layer that lost > 40 % of its height |
| budget "fix" that wasn't | with a 1,000-iteration NLGS budget the pile also exploded at release | the budget was a false lead: the explosion is the cap removal, not the solve | keep `deposit_max_checks = 0` on thick layers anyway (converged reactions); thin test beds may pin 100 |
| a rerun doubles the cost and loses the evidence | after `max_steps` the stage was wiped and rerun at half dt / double steps | old retry policy | only crashes/stalls retry; a settle that ran out of steps fails with its stage dir kept |
| a settled bed is silently re-settled under a new id | a campaign that names its bed by config re-settles after a settle default changed | the id hashes config + settle settings | name beds from the library (`@name`) or by id; the library pins the folder (`lab.py beds`) |
| a bed modified by accident | — | nothing stopped a stray write | registered beds are read-only and checksummed; `ensure_bed` refuses a bed whose manifest does not match |
| bed id changes under a running campaign | the campaign builds a *second* bed with a new id; old runs orphaned | a field was added to / removed from a hashed config dataclass (SettleSpec, BedSpec, …) | never change hashed dataclasses while beds or runs exist; configs pin values instead; `PHYSICS_VERSION` bump only when results must change |
| 8-vertex grains chatter longer than 12-vertex | more "moving" grains for longer on the same bed | fewer, larger faces → contact points jump more | 12 vertices (default); 20 settles equally well at ~1.4× the cost (see experiments) |
| smaller time step does not help rest | dt 1 ms: still chattering after 20 s simulated, twice the steps | the position rule ejects residual penetrations at gap/dt: a smaller dt kicks harder | keep dt 2 ms for settling |
| bed at rest explodes **with the cap on** (2026-10-30: it was NOT on — see the row "500-step check ran uncapped") | dt 1 ms bed: 0 grains moving at step 20,600, 54 at 2 m/s by 21,000, no change in the code at that moment | the window had lifted the speed limit at the first rest (step 20,500); the cap clips only the beginning-of-step velocity; the solve can still produce an ejection velocity that moves the grain within the step; once kicks outrun dissipation the pile blows | the cap delays, it does not prevent; save the state the moment the rest test passes (the pipeline does); stability levers under test: tolerance 1e-4, θ = 1, scrambled sweep order (docs/EXPERIMENTS_2026-10-29.md) |
| a settled bed held longer under the position rule bursts or explodes | thick test bed: rest at ~5,500, bursts at 8,000, explosion at ~9,600 with nothing changed; θ = 1 the same | the gap-law rest is metastable: residual penetrations within the NLGS tolerance are ejected at gap/dt and accumulate; **tolerance 1e-4 held 20,000 steps** (8× the cost per step; 3e-4 does not), while at 1e-3 a long hold is a coin flip (held once, exploded once) and the standard 500-step 500-step check held 4/4 seeds on the 1.5 m bed; 1e-4 needs the full budget and 2 ms to work; dt 3–4 ms and a 0.15 m/s cap explode too; at 4 m the window explodes every time at 1e-3 (two seeds, both saved from the snapshot), while the 3 m bed at 1e-4 held it at ~3× the step cost | the settle saves a snapshot at the first rest and falls back to it if the 500-step check explodes or overruns (card: `rest_window WARN`); tighten `tol` to 1e-4 only when a bed must be held; impact decks are immune — speed rule, zeroed velocities, micro-g: free grains move < 15 µm before first contact |
| bed at rest explodes when the solver budget changes | 3 m bed with a 1,000-iteration deposition budget: rest at step ~7,000, then 224 grains at 2 m/s within 1,000 steps of the polish switching to the full budget — cap still on (2026-10-30: the window was uncapped, so the budget switch is not proven to be a trigger on its own; the rule stays as a precaution) | any change of the contact solution's rules on a loaded deep pile (cap lift, budget switch, dt change) releases it | nothing changes between phases now; thick layers use the full budget from step one |
| NLGS NaN twice in a row | `nlgs_diverged` on both attempts, bed failed | friction 0.9 on rigid polyhedra under the position rule | friction ≤ 0.7; the retry policy is one crash retry, then fail |
| **500-step check ran uncapped** (every bed since 2026-10-28) | bed 1 of the six (1,853 boulders, 6 m): rest at step 19,800 after 15.4 h, 4 m/s within 100 steps, 12 m/s and grains 5 m away by step 21,200; in `settle_metrics.csv` the `capped` column turns 0 and `max_speed` jumps | `s.cap_speed()` sat behind the phase flag `if capped:` from the 10-28 rebuild; the 10-29 fix changed the comment and the call, not the guard; every window of every bed and every laptop experiment ran without the cap | the cap is applied on every step (beds.py, 2026-10-30); the first-rest snapshot still covers a window that blows for any other reason (`rest_window WARN`) |
| **boulder layer over small stones dies with a NaN at step ~150** | bed 5 of the six: `NLGS diverged at ITERATION 10` twice, log shows `GAP_SGR_CLB` and a 3 × 3 zero matrix for one contact; at step 100 a 16 cm overlap and a grain spinning at 1,100 rad/s (laptop layered beds: ≤ 12 mm, ≤ 30 rad/s at the same point); the deck banner says `NB BIG POLYR 17` | LMGC90 classes a polyhedron as "big" when its radius exceeds 4× the deck's MEAN radius (`threshold_big_POLYR`, mod_POLYR) and routes every pair touching a big one through `DETECTION_non_convex` instead of the common plane; 19,502 small stones 10–20 cm long made the mean radius 0.09 m, so 17 boulders above 0.36 m were big, and that path gave a contact with a zero local frame (Wnn = 0). No laptop deck ever had one (46 bed logs, 30 impact logs: all 0; the layered laptop beds sat at ratio 3.4) | `POLYR_SetThresholdBigPolyr(1e3)` before the bodies load (lmgcx.session, 2026-10-30); the pipeline refuses any settle or impact whose log still reports a big polyhedron (`big_polyr` failure); reproduced on the laptop the same day (`configs/experiments/bigpolyr_repro.toml` with `LMGCX_BIG_POLYR_RATIO=4`: 45 big polyhedra, spins 150 → 520 rad/s, non-finite velocity at step ~400, the tripwire names the cause; the same kind of deck with the study threshold runs at 13–40 rad/s) |
| boulders over velocity-law small stones sink into them | 2 m bed-5 probe: boulder–stone overlap 94 mm after 100 steps, 67 mm at the end (2.2 of the small stones' smallest semi-axis); the lab Mac's bed 5 showed 16 cm at step 100 | the speed rule only stops approach once a pair overlaps and detection lags a heavy boulder pressing into a soft bed with small stones; it never pushes back | the split contact rule: position rule on boulder–stone pairs (`boulder_fines_law`), overlap 0.0 mm on the same deck; beds 4–6 use it |
| bottom layer small stones fail QA on overlap | stone–stone 16 mm on small stones 10–20 cm long under a boulder layer = 0.53 of the smallest semi-axis, a FAIL under the 2026-10-30 rule; bed 5 would have failed after a week of settling | the speed rule among small stones leaves a loaded bed with small stones compressed; the position rule among small stones never rests over boulders | small stones overlap is graded where the craft can reach it (a grain in the top layer); buried pairs warn (`overlap_rel_fines_buried`); a bed graded FAIL under the older rule is re-graded from its settled layers (`QA_VERSION`) |
| a realistic-size-mix pile takes much longer to reach the median-move rest | 3 m bed-2 twin: median move 6–8 mm/s from step 5,100 to 7,900, 4–6 to 10,800, still above 3 at 11,500 (the even-size-mix bed 1 at 4.5 m rested at 4,500); mean speed 2–6 cm/s in bursts, a third of the grains "moving" | the whole polydisperse pile churns under the position rule's kicks — not only the small rattlers: the median of the load-bearing half (`d50_big`, beds.py 2026-10-31) tracks the overall median | budget ~2.5x the steps of a even-size-mix pile (`lab.py estimate` knows); a lower cap (0.15 m/s) only slows the rain-down 2x; inconclusive on the churn (`exp_bed2_3m_cap015`, stopped) |

## Impacts

| what happened | how it showed | cause | avoid / detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| spacecraft flies through a wall | box leaves the bed with no contact | no see-table row for that pair → pair never detected, no error | every pair the physics needs gets a row (scene `invisible_walls` pins it) |
| NaN at the first step | `Wnn = 0` | two FIXED bodies with a contact rule between them | frozen–frozen and frozen–floor pairs get no rule (scene `frozen_pairs`) |
| craft starts inside the terrain | `start_overlap` | aim point too close to a boulder top | the composer ray-casts every vertex and starts 2 cm clear; the check still runs |
| `left_patch` | the box left the patch low | patch too small for the bounce: at 8 cm/s and 30° every law arm left a 0.5 m patch; with friction 0.4 even 5 cm/s at 20° slid out | patch ≥ 1 m for grazing or fast arrivals (the 36-drop set use 1.2 m), `shell_mode = "adjacency"` |
| a drop declared over while the craft would touch again | bed 1, 4 cm/s at 40°: one touch at 0.5 s, "detached" at 1.3 s with e 0.44; the replay with detachment off grazed a boulder with a panel tip at 20.5 s | the look-ahead projected the rotating craft with a fixed spin axis; a free, asymmetric craft's axis wobbles (precession) and the panel tip drifted centimetres in 19 s | detachment requires that a sphere of the craft's reach on its exact ballistic arc clears the height map until the patch edge (detectors.reach_clear); `lab.py check-last-contact` replays each drop with detachment off; `lab.py campaign rerun` re-simulates recorded drops in place |
| a completed bounce voided as `left_patch` | 2 of 9 vertical drops at 8 cm/s on the 4 m bed (2026-10-31): last contact at 20–30 s, exit rising at 0.7 cm/s with 1.8 cm/s sideways, then 15–26 s of free flight until a panel tip reached the patch edge; `detached` never fired | the ballistic look-ahead treats every boulder as its bounding sphere, so a 1.47 m craft skimming 20 cm above a boulder field is never "clear" until it has risen above the tallest sphere — a minute at micro-g — and the drift out of the patch came first | a craft that crosses the patch edge airborne and rising, after the detachment time, is `detached` with its exit state (`contact.detached_how = "patch_edge"`); on the ground it stays `left_patch` (detectors.py, 2026-10-31) |
| the craft with panels open turned about the wrong point | the craft in the ParaView frames sat a constant 10.172 mm (along the craft's body x axis) from where craft.json puts it; the solver's gap to a boulder was 9.4 mm where an exact check of the craft.json geometry said 0 | LMGC90 centres a multi-contactor body on the volume-weighted centroid of its contactors' geometric volumes and ignores pre's per-piece "effective volumes"; light solar panels, heavy bus | an internal ballast box moves the volume centroid onto the centre of mass (impactors.ballast_piece); every drop records `setup.craft_geometry_error_m` and warns above 0.1 mm |
| a "massless" contactor counted as mass | with the first ballast pre's centre moved 7.17 mm and its inertia grew 7 % | pre recomputes a contactor's volume and inertia from its geometry when every inertia entry is below 1e-6 (rigidContactor3D) | give the ballast 2e-6 and take it off the craft's body; build_avatar asserts pre's centre and inertia equal the intended ones |
| a predicted touch that was not one | the exact check said a craft would touch a free grain 4 s after its run ended (b955841420ca); re-simulated, it never did, and the solver's gap never went below 2.3 mm | the check used craft.json's geometry while LMGC90 had the craft 10 mm elsewhere (row above) | flight.CraftShape uses LMGC90's body centre (lmgc90_centre for records before the ballast); compare any geometric check with the solver's own gaps (box_contacts.csv) |
| a drop declared over 1 s after its last touch, 10.08 mm from a free grain | first re-simulation with the exact end-of-drop test (8d1d0ee68da8) | a 1 cm margin assumes the grains are still; one second after an impact they are not | the margin around free grains grows with the fastest free grain's surface speed times the time ahead |
| `divide by zero / overflow / invalid value encountered in matmul` | RuntimeWarnings from finite matrices, results exact (4e-16 from a reference product) | numpy 2.0 with Apple's Accelerate BLAS raises spurious floating-point flags | `np.errstate(all="ignore")` around those products (flight.py) |
| patch too close to a wall | `target … is X m from a wall; the crop needs Y m` | bed margin: wall-affected grains | usable half-width from `describe`; sites inside it |
| spheres sink into boulders | SPPRx misses approaching contacts | LMGC90 sphere–polyhedron detection | all grains are polyhedra |
| random SIGBUS in detection | crash in `PRPRx_SelectProxTactors` | `polytopes_area_oob` in LMGC90 | `lab.py patch-lmgc90` (required on every machine) |

## Cohesion (gate D10, scene `cohesion`)

| what happened | how it showed | cause | avoid / detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| no bond at all | both cubes rose the free-flight distance | `cohn` is a **stress** (Pa) × detected contact area, not a force | give cohesion in Pa; the scene pins the bond = weight × 2 holding |
| bond for one step, then free | reaction = exactly the weight at step 1, then 0; status `slide` | tangential cohesion 0 → first tangential tug → `slide` → a sliding contact loses its bond | `coht = cohn` |
| bond on the floor carries no force | status `Mstck`, reaction 0 | polyhedron–plane contacts have no measured area | cohesion only between polyhedra (PRPRx); never rely on floor cohesion |
| cohesion silently equal to no cohesion in restarts | — | bonds form only at step 1 on virgin statuses | cohesive impact decks must reset the step counter and skip `RecupRloc` at step 1 (DECISIONS 2026-08-30) |

## Operations

| what happened | how it showed | avoid |
|---|---|---|
| a window blocks for days | shard window waiting on the bed lock of a bed another window is settling | `job.sh` asks `lab.py campaign ready` and revisits every 10 min |
| `pkill -f "exec bed-settle"` killed the waiting chain | the chain's own command text matched | stop runs by config name: `pkill -INT -f "lab.py bed --bed .*NAME.toml"` |
| OneDrive sync eats 1.6 cores on the laptop | every run-folder write synced | keep `runs/` outside OneDrive on a study machine (lab Mac: external drive) |
| two settles ran in the same bed folder | the log shows two `first rest` lines minutes apart; the slower run crashes at its end with `FileNotFoundError: 'state_rest1.h5' -> 'state.h5'` because the faster one had already promoted the snapshot; `[bed] stage 1 attempt 2 failed` | the registered bed is the faster run's and, same seed, bit-identical (`lab.py beds verify`: intact). `ensure_bed` holds a `.lock` in the bed folder; a parent started under older code did not. Never launch two chains for one bed; after any collision run `lab.py beds verify` |
| the 2-hourly report sync failed every time | `[sync] git rm -r -q --cached ... error: the following file has staged content different from both the file and the HEAD: reports/labmac/INDEX.md`, "they stay committed locally" in every job note; the morning sync worked | from the second sync on, INDEX.md differs from HEAD (its last local commit), from the remote tip (the previous push) and from the working copy at once, and `git rm --cached` refuses that; the full sync takes another path | the temporary index is built with `git add -A` (labops.py, 2026-10-30; tests/unit/test_sync.py reproduces the night) |
| ten windows waited eleven hours | `WAIT bed(s) still settling` every 10 min in job-07..16 until the cutoff | the plan promised bed 1 in 1.5 h; it took 15 h and blew up | the estimator is calibrated on the night; the slate keeps every window settling a bed of its own (three seeds per type) and only two windows wait for drops |
| settling at higher friction blows up the solver | "NLGS diverged at ITERATION 510", NaN contact reactions, at friction 0.8 (as the small stones landed) and 0.9 (twice in the boulder layer), 3 runs of 3; never at 0.6 | the contact solver loses its way on these angular grains when friction is high (not the big-POLYR path: that one diverges at iteration 10) | keep settle friction 0.6, the drops' friction (2026-11-01) |
